When my roof leaks, an advisor can tell me what to fix in five minutes.
A contractor takes a day and costs more, but the roof gets fixed.

AI works the same way.

Agents feel slow because they do more. They plan, use tools, check against the goal, and try again. Anthropic measured about 4× more tokens than chat.

So the question is *what result do I need?*

→ **Assistants help you understand.**
Answers, explanations, first drafts. Fast and cheap.
Copilot Chat, ChatGPT, Grok, Claude chat.

→ **Agents with a known goal get it done.**
Clear goal, mostly known steps: update tickets, prepare a report. You review the result.
Copilot Cowork, ChatGPT Work, Grok Bot, Snowflake CoWork.

→ **Agents for critical work must prove the result.**
Build, test, fix, test again. Like an engineer who tests the roof before the rain comes.
Claude Code, GitHub Copilot agent mode.

Many people use agents to send emails and make slides.
That is a contractor who sends a nice report. **The roof still leaks.**
Fine for pure admin. For the rest, fix the roof.

Which one do you use most today?
